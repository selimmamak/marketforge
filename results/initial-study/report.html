Evaluation assumptions

  • Features are available at the signal close. Targets run from the next open to a later open; training labels overlapping an evaluation boundary are removed.
  • Hyperparameters and the winning model are selected inside the research segment. Calibration and the final holdout do not tune the selection.
  • Simple-return forecast accuracy and trading profitability answer different questions. Volatility-scaled directional positions do not optimize portfolio covariance.
  • Adjusted historical prices are retrospective. A fixed present-day ticker list has survivorship and selection bias. Delisted securities and point-in-time corporate actions require a specialist data source.
  • Borrow costs, financing, cash yield, taxes, order-book liquidity, and nonlinear market impact are omitted. Shorting availability and idealized open fills are not guaranteed.
